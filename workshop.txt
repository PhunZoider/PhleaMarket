title=Phlea Market
description=[b]One survivor's junk.[/b]
description=A vending machine that allows other players (even in single player mode) to sell to one another.
description=
description=Hand it something, name a price, walk away. It holds your item until somebody buys it or the clock runs out, then pays you or hands it back.
description=
description=[b]Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3689006725]PhunMart 2[/url].[/b] This mod does nothing on its own.
description=
description=[h2]How It Works[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Open any Phlea Market, switch to [b]Sell[/b], pick something out of your bag and name a price
description=[*]It appears at [b]every[/b] Phlea Market in the world, not just the one you listed at
description=[*]Sold? The money is waiting for you. Unsold? So is your gear.
description=[*]Collect either from any machine, and relist in one click
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Your Gear Comes Back As Your Gear[/h2]
description=Items keep their condition. A worn out axe sells as a worn out axe and comes back as one, so the market cannot be used to launder damaged kit into new kit. Buyers see what they are actually paying for.
description=
description=Magazines, attachments and bag contents are handed back to you when you list something, and the machine tells you before it takes anything.
description=
description=[h2]Selling In Bulk[/h2]
description=A hundred shotgun shells is one listing with a price per shell, not a hundred listings. Buyers take as many as they want and the rest stays up.
description=
description=[h2]Finding Things[/h2]
description=The browse screen shows one tile per kind of item, with the cheapest price and how many are to be had. Open it to see who is selling and for how much, cheapest first, then oldest first, so undercutting by nothing does not jump the queue.
description=
description=Filter by category, or search by name. Start a search with @ to search sellers instead, so @steve finds everything Steve has up.
description=
description=[h2]Knowing What To Charge[/h2]
description=Naming a price is the hardest part of any market, so the machine tells you what you need. What the same item is listed at now, what it has recently sold for, and what you asked the last time you sold one. It opens at that last price.
description=
description=You are told the moment something sells, or given a summary next time you log in if you were away.
description=
description=[h2]For Server Admins[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Two fees, both configurable[/b]: a listing fee and a sale commission, each a percentage or a flat amount. A flat fee asks the same for a nail as for a gold bar, so percentages are the default
description=[*][b]Three listing lengths[/b] with their own fee multipliers: waiting longer for a buyer costs more
description=[*][b]Commission is where money leaves the world[/b], which makes it the dial to reach for on an inflating server
description=[*][b]Limits that matter[/b]: listings per account, stack sizes, price floor and ceiling, so nobody uses the market as free storage
description=[*][b]A ledger[/b] of every listing, sale, cancellation and expiry, for the first time two players disagree about who was paid what
description=[*][b]Admin tools[/b] to view, sort, search and pull listings, returning the goods to their owner rather than destroying them
description=[*][b]Blacklist by item, category or tag[/b]: no food, no medical, no whatever your server does not want traded
description=[*][b]Wallet caps are respected[/b]: proceeds that will not fit wait until the seller has room, so the market cannot be used to get around them
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Playing Alone[/h2]
description=Turn on simulated sellers and the market fills with listings from survivors who do not exist. Their stock is drawn from what PhunMart's own machines sell, at prices around what those machines charge, so it stays anchored to your economy even if you have rewritten every price in the game.
description=
description=Also useful on a quiet server, where a market that is technically live and practically empty is worse than no market at all.
description=
description=[h2]FAQs[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Do I need PhunMart 2?[/b] Yes. Phlea Market uses its wallet, its shop window and its admin tools.
description=[*][b]Where do the listings live?[/b] On the server, globally. List at one machine, collect at another.
description=[*][b]What happens if I die?[/b] Nothing. Listings and returns belong to your account, not your character.
description=[*][b]What if my wallet is full when something sells?[/b] You are paid what fits and the rest is held until you have room.
description=[*][b]Can I get my item back?[/b] Cancel any listing that has not sold. The listing fee is not refunded.
description=[*][b]Can I add it to an existing save?[/b] Yes.
description=[/list]
